# Maintenance task catalogue

Use this to build the task list during setup. Include only tasks for systems the house actually has. Favour fewer, well-timed tasks: one busy person runs this house. Seasonal timing assumes a place with cold winters; shift or drop tasks for the user's climate (no freeze tasks where it doesn't freeze).

| Task | System | Timing | Lead time / prep |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test smoke and CO alarms | Safety | Monthly | — |
| Replace smoke/CO alarm batteries | Safety | Yearly (pick a memorable date, e.g. a clock change) | Buy batteries |
| Replace or check furnace filter | Furnace | Every 1–3 months (1" filters ~1–3 mo, 4–5" media ~6–12 mo) | Buy the right size the week before |
| Furnace service | Furnace | Yearly, early fall | Book 2–3 weeks ahead; fall gets busy |
| Check salt level, top up to about half | Water softener | Monthly | Keep a spare bag |
| Check for salt bridge (broom handle) | Water softener | Every 2–3 months | — |
| Test water hardness | Water softener | Twice a year | Buy test strips |
| Test sump pump (bucket of water in the pit) | Sump pump | Spring, before the thaw; again in fall | — |
| Flush water heater | Water heater | Yearly | — |
| Clean gutters | Exterior | Late fall after leaves drop; spring | Ladder or book someone |
| Shut off and drain outdoor faucets | Plumbing | Fall: before first freeze | Know where the inside shutoff is |
| Cover or protect the AC unit | AC | Fall: after last warm spell, before snow | Cover or plywood |
| Uncover AC, service it | AC | Spring: before first hot week | Book service in spring |
| Reverse ceiling fans | Fans | Fall (clockwise, low) and spring | — |
| Check the main water shutoff turns | Plumbing | Yearly | — |
| Clean dryer vent duct | Dryer | Yearly | — |
| Clean dishwasher filter | Dishwasher | Monthly | — |
| Check weatherstripping and caulking | Exterior | Fall | — |

## Seasonal windows (cold-winter defaults)

- **Fall / before first freeze:** typically late October to late November. Flag 2–3 weeks before the usual first hard frost for the user's area, using a web search for the area's typical date when unsure.
- **Spring:** typically April. Flag sump pump and AC prep as snow melts.

When a seasonal task's window opens, set its Next due to a concrete date inside the window so it shows up like any other due task.
